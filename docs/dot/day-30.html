当地新闻

原题与完整提示

Describe a piece of local news that people are interested in You should say: What it was about Where you saw/heard it Who was involved And explain why people were interested in it

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A piece of local news that could attract a lot of interest was an announcement that a public library would extend its evening opening hours. The report explained that a study area would stay available later, giving residents more flexibility after work or classes. I saw the announcement in a local online news article. It involved the library staff, the local authorities supporting the service and residents who wanted a quiet place to read or study. The report also described practical details, such as which area would be open and how visitors could use it. I think people were interested because the change affected ordinary life directly. Not everyone has a quiet home environment, and some people finish work too late to use a library during normal daytime hours. A relatively small change in a public service could therefore create a useful opportunity. What made the story appealing to me was its practical scale. It was not about a dramatic event or a famous person. It was about making an existing resource more accessible. As a student, I can understand why a reliable, calm place to study would matter to people.

中文对照

可能吸引许多人关注的一则本地新闻，是公共图书馆宣布延长晚间开放时间。报道解释部分学习区域会开放更晚，让居民下班或下课后更灵活使用。 我在一篇本地网络新闻报道中看到了这则公告，涉及图书馆工作人员、支持服务的地方部门，以及想找安静地方阅读学习的居民。报道也有具体信息，比如开放哪一区、如何使用。 我认为人们关注，是因为变化直接影响日常。不是人人家里安静，有些人下班太晚，无法在白天正常时段去图书馆。所以公共服务的小变化也能带来有用机会。 吸引我的是，这则新闻着眼于日常生活中的具体问题：不是戏剧事件或名人，而是让已有资源更易获得。作为学生，我理解可靠安静的学习空间为什么重要。

关键词

a public library · evening opening hours · local residents · accessible services · a quiet place to study

待确认素材与观点

  • 图书馆公告、网络文章及具体服务变化均为未确认候选新闻，不声称已联网核验或已在用户城市发生。

近期改变

原题与完整提示

Describe a change that you made recently You should say: What the change was What caused the change What you did for the change And explain how you feel about the change

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A recent change I'd describe is keeping my phone out of reach during a focused study session. Previously, I would leave it beside my laptop, so even a small notification could interrupt what I was doing. The reason for changing was that I wanted my English preparation to be more deliberate. Simply sitting at the desk for a long time did not guarantee useful work. If I checked messages repeatedly, it became harder to follow a difficult sentence or stay with a task that needed concentration. I started by switching off unnecessary notifications and leaving the phone away from the desk. I also gave each study session a small, clear goal and checked the phone during a planned break. At first, the habit felt slightly uncomfortable because reaching for it was almost automatic. What I like about the change is that it is simple. It does not require a complicated application or a perfect timetable. It just removes one source of distraction. I would describe the feeling as calmer and more purposeful: I can judge a session by what I understood or completed, rather than only by how long I was sitting there.

中文对照

我想描述的近期变化，是专注学习时把手机放到够不到的位置。原先放在笔记本旁，小通知也会打断。 改变是为了让英语备考更有目的。在书桌前坐很久，并不保证学习有效；反复查消息会让理解难句、专注任务更困难。 我先关掉不必要通知，把手机放离桌面；再给每段学习设小而清晰的目标，在计划好的休息时看手机。最初略不习惯，因为伸手拿手机几乎成了下意识的动作。 我喜欢它的简单：无需复杂软件或完美时间表，只减少一个干扰来源。我会把感觉描述为更平静、更有目标；可以用真正理解或完成了什么评价学习，而不只计算坐了多久。

关键词

out of reach · unnecessary notifications · a clear goal · a planned break · more purposeful

待确认素材与观点

  • 手机原习惯、实际改变、执行步骤和效果均为完整候选，不视为用户已实施学习改进。
