很久没收到回复的信息

原题与完整提示

Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but received no reply for a long time You should say: Who you sent it to What the message/email was about Whether you finally received the reply And explain how you felt about the experience

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An occasion when I waited a long time for an email reply involved a conference organiser. I had sent a question about a practical arrangement for an academic event, because I wanted to know what I needed to prepare before travelling. The message was quite short. I explained the issue, gave the relevant details and asked for clarification. I expected an answer within a few days, but almost a week passed without one. That was frustrating because I could not finish that part of my planning confidently. At first, I kept checking my inbox, which did not help. I then looked through the event information again to make sure the answer was not already available. After that, I sent one polite follow-up instead of several repeated messages. Eventually, I received a reply with the information I needed. The organiser explained that they had been dealing with many requests. I felt relieved, and the delay became easier to understand. The experience reminded me to leave extra time for communication and not assume that a slow answer means someone is deliberately ignoring me.

中文对照

一次长时间等邮件回复的经历，与会议组织者有关。我询问学术活动的实际安排，因为想知道出行前需要准备什么。 消息很短，我说明问题、给相关信息并请求澄清。原以为几天内有答复，却快一周没有消息，令人烦躁，因为无法放心完成那部分规划。 开始我反复查收件箱，并没有帮助。后来又看了活动信息，确认其中还没有现成的答案。之后只礼貌跟进一次，而非连续催促。 最终，我收到了需要的信息。组织者解释说正在处理很多请求，我松了一口气，也更能理解这次延迟。这段经历提醒我，应给沟通留出额外时间，不要把慢回复自动当作故意忽视。

关键词

a conference organiser · clarification · almost a week · one polite follow-up · leave extra time

待确认素材与观点

  • 发给组织者的邮件、等待时长、跟进过程及对方解释均为候选故事；仅赴学术会议的背景已确认。

Why do people always tell children stories with animals?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）
