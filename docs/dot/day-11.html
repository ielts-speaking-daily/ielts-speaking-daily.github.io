不喜欢的短途旅行

原题与完整提示

Describe a short trip you often take but do not like You should say: Where you go When you go there Why you go there And explain why you do not like the trip

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A short trip I'd describe as necessary but not enjoyable is the journey from my apartment to the university when I need to attend a meeting. I take a bus, and I make the trip regularly during the study term. I go there because some research discussions are easier to have in person. We can look at the same materials and ask questions immediately. So the destination is useful, even though the journey itself isn't something I look forward to. The main problem is that the travelling time can be unpredictable. If the road is busy, a short distance may take much longer than expected. I also find it harder to read or concentrate when the bus is crowded and people are moving around me. I don't dislike travelling in general; I enjoyed having the chance to visit Europe. This trip feels different because it's repeated and mainly serves a practical purpose. I'd prefer a more reliable journey or the option of an online meeting when being there in person isn't really necessary. That would help me use my time more efficiently.

中文对照

一段有必要但让我不太享受的短途行程，是需要参加会议时，从公寓去大学的路程。我乘公交车，并在学期中经常往返。 我去大学，是因为有些研究讨论当面进行更方便。我们能一起看相同的材料，马上提问。因此，去学校是有用的，尽管我并不期待这段路程。 主要问题是行程时间可能不稳定。道路繁忙时，短距离也可能花很久。公交拥挤、周围人移动时，我也更难阅读或集中注意力。 我并不是不喜欢旅行本身，我很享受能去欧洲参观的机会。这段行程不同，因为它重复发生，主要是为了办事。我更希望行程稳定，或者在不必现场参加时可以线上开会。这样能更有效地利用时间。

关键词

apartment to university · regular bus journey · meetings · unpredictable traffic · efficient use of time

待确认素材与观点

  • 当前居所与学校之间的通勤、交通方式、频率、会议和不喜欢的理由均为候选；不要用一次欧洲行硬替代经常往返短途题

受欢迎的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a popular person You should say: Who this person is What kind of person he or she is When you see him/her normally And explain why you think this person is popular

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A popular person I'd like to describe is my supervisor. I'm a master's student working in AI and robotics, so I normally see them during research discussions and meetings at university. They are a knowledgeable person, but what makes them approachable is the way they speak to students. They listen carefully and explain difficult points without making someone feel foolish for asking a basic question. They can be serious about the work while still creating a relaxed atmosphere. For example, if a student is stuck on a problem, they ask what has already been tried and help identify the next step. That feels more useful than simply pointing out that the result is wrong. They also give quieter students a chance to speak, instead of letting the most confident person dominate the discussion. I think they are popular because students feel respected and supported. I'm generally quiet, especially around people I don't know well, so that kind of atmosphere matters to me. Their popularity seems to come from consistent behaviour rather than trying to entertain everyone. To me, that's a more meaningful kind of popularity.

中文对照

我想描述的一位受欢迎的人是我的导师。我是研究人工智能和机器人的硕士生，所以通常会在学校的研究讨论和会议中见到导师。 导师知识丰富，而他们与学生交流的方式让人觉得很容易亲近。他们会认真倾听、解释难点，不会让学生因为问了一个基础问题就觉得自己很笨。他们对待工作很认真，同时也能营造轻松的气氛。 例如，学生遇到问题时，导师会先问已经尝试过哪些方法，再帮助确定下一步该怎么做。这比单纯指出结果错了更有用。他们也会给比较安静的学生发言机会，而不是让最自信的人主导整个讨论。 我认为导师受欢迎，是因为学生能够感受到尊重和支持。我平时比较安静，尤其是在不太熟悉的人面前，所以这样的气氛对我很重要。导师的好人缘似乎来自一贯的待人方式，而不是刻意逗所有人开心。对我来说，这是一种更有意义的受欢迎。

关键词

supervisor · research meetings · listen carefully · quieter students · respect

待确认素材与观点

  • 导师受欢迎程度、见面频率、具体指导和分配发言的做法是授权候选，待用户确认；尚未作答。

原题与完整提示

Should documentary films be completely truthful?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Documentaries should be honest about the facts they present. They can select and organize material, because no film can include everything, but they shouldn't invent events or mislead the audience through editing. If a scene is reconstructed, that should be clear. Viewers need to distinguish evidence from interpretation so they can trust the film without treating one perspective as the whole story.

中文对照

纪录片应诚实呈现事实。可以选择组织材料，因为电影不可能包含一切，但不应编事件或通过剪辑误导。若场景是重现，应明确。观众需区分证据与解读，才能信任影片又不把一个视角当全部。

关键词

facts · editing · reconstruction · interpretation

待确认素材与观点

  • 观点是否符合用户看法待确认；尚未作答或录音验证
