印象深刻的聚会

原题与完整提示

Describe a party for a special event that impressed you a lot You should say: When and where it was held What the party was held for How many people attended it And explain why it impressed you a lot

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A party that impressed me was a small celebration after a group of classmates finished a demanding university project. It took place one evening at a quiet restaurant near campus, and about eight people attended. The purpose wasn't to celebrate a competition prize or a major public achievement. We were simply pleased that we had completed the work and could finally relax together. We ordered some food, sat around one table, and talked about the project as well as things unrelated to study. What made the evening memorable was how different everyone seemed outside the working environment. People who had been serious during meetings started telling funny stories. We also thanked one another for small contributions that hadn't received much attention during the busy period. I'm quiet and rational, but I become more talkative around familiar people. So a small gathering suited me better than a loud event with lots of strangers. I didn't need to compete for a chance to speak, and I could actually hear the conversation. The party impressed me because it turned a shared task into a warmer relationship, without requiring expensive decorations or a complicated programme.

中文对照

让我印象深刻的一次聚会，是一群同学完成一个有难度的大学项目后举行的小型庆祝。聚会是在某天晚上，地点是校园附近一家安静的餐馆，大约有八个人参加。 我们并不是为了庆祝获得比赛奖项或取得什么重大的公开成就。大家只是很高兴终于完成了工作，可以一起放松一下。我们点了一些食物，围坐在一张桌子旁，既聊了项目，也聊了与学习无关的事情。 那天晚上难忘的地方在于，离开工作环境后，每个人似乎都变得不太一样。平时开会时很严肃的人开始讲有趣的故事。我们也感谢了彼此在忙碌期间做出的那些不太受关注的小贡献。 我比较安静、理性，但在熟人面前会更健谈。因此，相比有很多陌生人的吵闹活动，这样的小聚会更适合我。我不需要争抢发言机会，也能听清大家在说什么。它让我印象深刻，是因为这次聚会让一起完成任务的经历变成了更亲近的关系，而且并不需要昂贵的装饰或复杂的活动安排。

关键词

project finished · eight classmates · quiet restaurant · conversation · closer relationships

待确认素材与观点

  • 项目完成后的聚会、时间地点、约八人、具体互动均为完整授权候选，非已确认经历；性格已确认。

有健康习惯和作息的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person you know who has healthy habits and a healthy daily routine You should say: Who this person is How you got to know each other What he/she does to keep this lifestyle And explain how you feel about him/her

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A person I know with healthy habits is a friend from university. We got to know each other through classes and shared study activities, and their routine stood out because it was steady rather than extreme. They try to go to bed at a reasonable time and avoid leaving every task until late at night. During the day, they take short breaks from the computer and go for a walk when possible. They also prepare simple meals instead of depending on snacks whenever they are busy. What I find sensible is that they don't treat health as a competition. If a deadline makes one day difficult, they return to their normal habits afterwards rather than give up completely. They plan ahead, but still leave room for meeting friends or enjoying a relaxing evening. I respect that approach because studying can make it easy to spend a long time sitting and forget basic needs. Their routine shows that looking after yourself doesn't require a complicated system. Small actions repeated over time can be more realistic than a dramatic change that lasts only a few days. I'd like to learn from that consistency.

中文对照

我认识的一位有健康习惯的人，是大学里的一位朋友。我们通过课程和一起学习认识，对方的作息让我印象深刻，因为这些习惯很稳定，并不是靠极端做法维持的。 这位朋友尽量在合理的时间睡觉，避免把所有任务都拖到深夜。白天会短暂离开电脑休息一下，有机会就出去散步。即使忙碌，也会准备简单的饭菜，而不是总靠零食解决吃饭的问题。 我觉得很合理的一点是，对方不把健康当作一场比赛。如果某天因为赶任务而难以保持作息，之后就恢复平时的习惯，而不是彻底放弃。对方会提前安排事情，但仍然给见朋友或度过一个轻松的晚上留出空间。 我很欣赏这种做法，因为学习时很容易长时间坐着，忘记自己的基本需要。这套作息说明，照顾好自己并不需要复杂的系统。长期坚持的小行动，可能比只持续几天的大幅改变更现实。我想学习的正是这种持续性。

关键词

university friend · steady routine · short breaks · simple meals · consistency

待确认素材与观点

  • 朋友身份关系、认识方式、全部作息和用户评价均为授权候选，需替换或确认；不登记健康习惯真实样本。
