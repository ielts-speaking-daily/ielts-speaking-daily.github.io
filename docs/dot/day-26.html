包含动物的故事或书

原题与完整提示

Describe a story/book with animals in it You should say: What animals are in it What the story/book is about Why you read the story/book And explain what you think of this story/book

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A book with animals in it that I'd like to describe is The Little Prince. It follows a young prince who leaves his small planet and visits different places before arriving on Earth. Two memorable animals in the story are a fox and a snake. The fox is especially important because it helps the prince think about friendship and the time we give to someone. The snake appears in a different, more mysterious role. Although the book looks simple at first, the characters encourage readers to consider relationships, responsibility and what makes something meaningful. I chose to read it because it was short enough to approach without feeling overwhelmed, but had ideas worth returning to. A friend recommended it as more than a children's story, which made me curious to give it a try. What I find attractive is the contrast between the straightforward language and the questions beneath it. The animals are not just decoration; they help express ideas that might sound too abstract in an ordinary explanation. I would see it as a book to read slowly and think about, rather than rush through for the plot alone.

中文对照

我想描述的有动物的书是《小王子》。故事讲年轻王子离开小星球、访问不同地方，最终来到地球。狐狸和蛇是其中两种令人难忘的动物。 狐狸特别重要，因为它帮助王子思考友谊，以及我们为一个人投入的时间。蛇则扮演更神秘的角色。书起初看似简单，但人物让读者思考关系、责任和什么赋予事物意义。 我选择读它，是因为篇幅短，不让人觉得难以开始，却有值得反复思考的想法。一位朋友推荐，说它不只是儿童故事，让我好奇想试读。 我觉得吸引人的，是直白语言与背后问题的反差。动物不只是装饰，而是帮助表达普通解释中可能过于抽象的想法。我会把它当作值得慢读、思考的书，而非只为情节匆匆读完。

关键词

The Little Prince · a fox and a snake · friendship · responsibility · simple language, deeper questions

待确认素材与观点

  • 用户是否读过《小王子》未确认；读过及朋友推荐的叙述均为用户授权的完整候选，不能登记为真实阅读经历。

保护环境的法律

原题与完整提示

Describe a law on environmental protection You should say: What it is How you first learned about it Who benefits from it And explain how you feel about this law

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A law on environmental protection I'd like to discuss is China's law aimed at preventing and controlling air pollution. In broad terms, it creates responsibilities for controlling emissions and protecting the quality of the air people breathe. The first time I paid attention to it was through a news report explaining how pollution affects ordinary life. That made the idea of environmental law feel practical rather than distant. I realised that cleaner air depends not only on individual choices, but also on rules for businesses and public authorities. The people who benefit include local residents, workers and anyone spending time outdoors. Children and people who are more sensitive to poor air quality may especially need that protection. Businesses can also benefit from clearer expectations when the rules are applied fairly. I support the purpose of the law, but I think implementation matters as much as the written words. Requirements need to be understandable, monitoring needs to be reliable, and responsibility should not fall only on individuals. For me, a good environmental law connects a clear public goal with practical steps that people and organisations can actually follow.

中文对照

我想谈中国旨在防治空气污染的法律。大体而言，它规定了控制排放、保障人们呼吸到的空气质量的责任。 我最初留意它，是通过一篇解释污染如何影响日常生活的新闻。这让我觉得环境法律很实际，而非遥远的概念。我意识到，清洁空气不只依赖个人选择，也需要针对企业和公共部门的规则。 受益者包括居民、劳动者以及在户外活动的人。孩子和对空气质量更敏感的人尤其需要保护。公平实施时，明确要求也有益于企业。 我支持法律目的，但认为法律的落实与写在纸上的规定同样重要。要求要容易理解，监测要可靠，责任不应只落在个人身上。在我看来，好的环境法律把明确的公共目标与个人和组织真正能够执行的具体步骤联系起来。

关键词

air pollution · controlling emissions · a news report · local residents · implementation

待确认素材与观点

  • 首次通过新闻了解该法的个人经历和感受为候选，未确认；不编造条款、日期或处罚数额。
