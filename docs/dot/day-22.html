在团队中工作

原题与完整提示

Describe a time when you worked in a group You should say: What you did Who you worked with What problems you faced And explain why you worked in the group

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A time I worked in a group was during a university project related to robotics. I worked with three other students, and our aim was to create a small demonstration in which a robot responded to information from a computer program. We divided the work according to our strengths. Some people focused on the robot's movement, while others dealt with the program and testing. I helped with the coding and tried to make the information passed between the different parts clear. The main difficulty was that each part could work separately but still fail when we combined them. We had also made different assumptions about the format of the data. To deal with that, we agreed on a simple shared example and tested one connection at a time. Regular short discussions helped us notice problems before they became larger. We worked as a group because the task required more than one type of knowledge, and sharing the work made it manageable. I found the process useful because I had to explain my thinking instead of only solve problems privately. I'm fairly quiet, but a clear task gives me something concrete to contribute. The experience showed me that cooperation includes careful listening as well as technical ability.

中文对照

我参加团队工作的一次经历，是大学里的一个机器人项目。我与另外三名学生合作，目标是做一个小型演示，让机器人能够根据电脑程序传来的信息作出反应。 我们按照各自的优势分工。有些人负责机器人的运动，另一些人负责程序和测试。我帮忙写代码，并努力让不同部分之间的信息传递更清楚。 主要困难是，每个部分单独都能工作，但组合起来仍然可能出问题。我们对数据格式也作出了不同的假设。为了解决这个问题，我们确定了一个简单的共同示例，每次只测试一个连接。定期进行简短讨论，也帮助我们在问题变大之前就发现它们。 我们之所以组队，是因为任务需要不止一种知识，而分担工作能让任务更容易处理。我觉得这个过程很有用，因为我必须解释自己的思路，而不只是独自解决问题。我比较安静，但明确的任务让我有具体的事情可以贡献。这次经历让我看到，合作不仅需要技术能力，也需要认真倾听。

关键词

robotics project · four students · coding · data format · shared testing

待确认素材与观点

  • 机器人团队项目、四人、分工、接口问题和解决经过全部为授权候选；不宣称真实项目、已完成机器人演示或掌握技能。

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An important decision I made was to plan for a PhD abroad. I started thinking seriously about that goal about a year ago, and it has become a direction for what I want to do after my master's degree. My current subject is artificial intelligence, especially robotics. When considering the next step, I thought about both the research I wanted to pursue and the kind of education I hoped to experience. Studying abroad appealed to me because I wanted to broaden my perspective and learn in a more varied environment. I made the decision by comparing it with the option of ending my formal studies after the master's. I considered the time and effort involved, rather than only the attractive parts. The result so far is a clearer goal and a reason to take my English preparation seriously. The decision matters because I want to deepen my research in embodied AI and help bring general-purpose intelligent robots into real-world use. That is a long-term aim, and I know it will involve difficult work. Having a direction doesn't remove that uncertainty, but it helps me choose what to focus on. For me, the importance lies in connecting further education with a purpose I genuinely care about.

中文对照

我作出的一个重要决定，是计划去海外读博士。大约一年前，我开始认真考虑这个目标，而它已经成为我硕士毕业后的一个发展方向。 我现在的专业是人工智能，尤其关注机器人。考虑下一步时，我既思考了想继续开展什么研究，也思考了希望接受怎样的教育。海外学习吸引我，是因为我希望拓宽视野，在更加多元的环境中学习。 作决定时，我把这条路与硕士毕业后不再继续正规学业的选择作了比较。我考虑了需要投入的时间和精力，而不只是它有吸引力的一面。目前的结果是，目标更加清晰，也让我有理由认真对待英语备考。 这个决定很重要，因为我希望深化具身智能研究，帮助通用智能机器人走向实际应用。这是一个长期目标，我知道其中会有艰难的工作。有了方向并不能消除不确定性，但能帮助我决定把注意力放在哪里。对我来说，它的重要性在于把继续深造与一个我真正关心的目标联系起来。

关键词

PhD abroad · about a year · broader perspective · English preparation · embodied AI

喜欢的现场体育赛事

原题与完整提示

Describe a live sports event you watched and liked You should say: What it was When and where you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why you liked it

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A live sports event I enjoyed was a basketball match between two student teams at university. I watched it last semester on a campus court with two classmates. It wasn't a professional competition with famous athletes, but there was a lively atmosphere. Supporters stood near the court, and it was easy to see the players communicating and reacting to what happened. Because we were there in person, the changes in pace felt more immediate than they would on a screen. The match stayed fairly close, so neither side could relax. One team played more carefully near the end, passed the ball patiently, and created a good chance instead of rushing. I liked watching how the players adjusted their decisions under pressure. I enjoyed the event because it combined a clear shared goal with teamwork. I didn't need to be an expert to appreciate a well-timed pass or someone helping a teammate recover from a mistake. Going with familiar classmates also made the experience comfortable. We could talk about the game afterwards without turning it into a loud social occasion. For me, it was an enjoyable break from studying and a practical example of cooperation happening in real time.

中文对照

我喜欢的一次现场体育赛事，是大学里两支学生队伍之间的篮球比赛。上学期，我和两位同学在校园球场观看了这场比赛。 它并不是有著名运动员参加的职业比赛，但现场气氛很活跃。支持者站在场边，我们可以很清楚地看到球员怎样交流、怎样对场上的情况作出反应。因为是在现场，比赛节奏的变化比隔着屏幕观看更直接。 双方的比分一直很接近，所以谁都不能放松。有一支队伍在接近结束时打得更加谨慎，耐心传球，创造出一个好机会，而不是急着出手。我喜欢观察球员怎样在压力下调整自己的决策。 我喜欢这场比赛，是因为它把明确的共同目标与团队合作结合起来。即使不是专家，我也能欣赏一次时机恰好的传球，或者队友怎样帮助失误的球员重新调整。和熟悉的同学一起去，也让我感到自在。赛后我们可以聊聊比赛，而不必把它变成一场吵闹的社交活动。对我来说，这既是学习之余一次愉快的休息，也是在现场看到团队合作展开的具体例子。

关键词

live basketball · campus court · two classmates · close match · teamwork

待确认素材与观点

  • 校园现场篮球观看、上学期、两同学、赛况与喜欢原因全为授权候选。明确live现场而非电视或网络直播；不虚构用户看过正式大赛。
