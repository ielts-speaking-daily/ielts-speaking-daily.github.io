遇到的科技问题

原题与完整提示

Describe a challenging technological problem you faced You should say: What the problem was When and where you faced it How challenging it was And explain how you solved it

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A technological problem I'd describe involved a programme that suddenly stopped working on my laptop. It was related to a small robotics project, and I noticed the problem while working at home one afternoon. The programme had worked before, but after a software update it produced an error as soon as I started it. At first, I did not know whether the problem was in my own code or in one of the packages it depended on. That uncertainty made it frustrating, because changing several things at once could make the situation even harder to understand. I decided to work through it systematically. I read the error message carefully, checked what had changed and tested a much smaller piece of code. That helped me identify a mismatch between package versions. I created a separate environment, installed compatible versions and ran the small test again before returning to the whole programme. The programme finally worked, and I felt relieved. More importantly, the experience showed me why it helps to keep clear notes and isolate a problem instead of guessing. It was challenging, but it became manageable once I broke it into steps.

中文对照

我想描述一次笔记本上程序突然不能运行的问题。它与小型机器人项目有关，是某天下午在家工作时发现的。 程序原先正常，软件更新后，一启动就报错。开始不清楚是自己的代码，还是依赖的软件包出了问题。这种不确定令人烦躁，因为同时改很多地方可能让问题更难理解。 我决定按步骤检查：认真阅读错误信息、检查哪些地方发生了变化，并测试一段更小的代码。这帮助找到软件包版本不匹配。我创建独立环境，安装兼容版本，先运行小测试，再回到完整程序。 程序终于正常，我松了口气。更重要的是，这让我认识到记录清楚、隔离问题比猜测更有帮助。虽然困难，但拆成步骤后就能处理。

关键词

a software update · an error message · package versions · a separate environment · systematic testing

待确认素材与观点

  • 项目、报错、更新、排查步骤及发生时间地点均为候选事件，未登记为用户真实经历。

原题与完整提示

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An advertisement I'd like to describe features Yao Ming, the former Chinese basketball player. It is a public-awareness advertisement about protecting sharks rather than selling a normal consumer product. The message is that demand for shark-fin products can lead to harm to these animals. You can see this kind of campaign material online, including short videos and shared images. The version I have in mind uses a familiar public figure to encourage people to think about what their purchases support. Because Yao Ming is widely recognised, his presence makes the message easier to notice and remember. What I like is that the advertisement connects an everyday consumer choice with a consequence that may be far away and normally invisible. It does not need a complicated story to make the basic point. However, I think a famous face should draw attention to the issue, not replace evidence or a proper explanation. Overall, I find it more worthwhile than an advertisement that only encourages unnecessary spending. It tries to change behaviour for a wider purpose, and that makes the celebrity's involvement feel relevant rather than simply decorative.

中文对照

我想描述一则由前中国篮球运动员姚明出演的广告。它宣传保护鲨鱼，而非销售普通消费品，传达的是对鱼翅产品的需求可能伤害这些动物。 这类宣传材料能在网上见到，包括短视频和分享图片。我想到的版本借助熟悉的公众人物，让人思考购买行为在支持什么。姚明广为人知，因此信息更易被注意和记住。 我喜欢它把日常消费选择，与通常看不见、距离很远的后果连接起来，不需要复杂故事就能说明基本道理。不过，我认为名人应吸引关注，而不应取代证据或充分解释。 总体而言，我认为它比只鼓励不必要消费的广告更值得关注。它希望为更广的目的改变行为，因此，名人的参与显得与主题相关，而不只是起装饰作用。

关键词

Yao Ming · a public-awareness advertisement · protecting sharks · consumer choices · a wider purpose

待确认素材与观点
