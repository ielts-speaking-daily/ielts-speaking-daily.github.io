I like that the discussion is serious without making me afraid to ask questions. I feel both respect and gratitude towards them. Instead of pushing the most expensive model, they showed me which differences would actually affect my work. I also had a question about setting the computer up. They took some time to demonstrate the basic steps and made sure I understood what to do. I didn't feel rushed, even when I asked another question.
