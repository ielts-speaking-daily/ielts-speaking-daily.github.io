钦佩的运动员

原题与完整提示

Describe a successful sportsperson you admire You should say: Who he/she is What you know about him/her What he/she is like in real life What achievement he/she has made

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A successful sportsperson I admire is Ma Long, the Chinese table tennis player. I know him mainly through sports news and videos of international competitions. He has won Olympic gold medals and major international titles, so his achievements are impressive even to someone who doesn't follow every match. What interests me most is that success in table tennis depends on quick reactions and careful decisions, not just physical strength. I don't know him personally, so my impression of his life outside competition comes from interviews and other public appearances. He seems calm, disciplined, and serious about improving. On the court, he can change his approach when an opponent creates a new problem, which makes his matches interesting to watch. The quality I admire is his ability to stay focused under pressure. A strong player still makes mistakes, but has to move on immediately rather than let one point affect the next. That is relevant to study and research too. I wouldn't compare my work directly with elite sport, but his patience and consistency offer a useful example of how progress requires more than a brief period of enthusiasm.

中文对照

我钦佩的一位成功运动员是中国乒乓球选手马龙。我主要通过体育新闻和国际比赛的视频了解他。 他赢得过奥运金牌和重要国际赛事的冠军，所以即使是不关注每一场比赛的人，也会对他的成就印象深刻。我最感兴趣的是，乒乓球比赛中的成功依靠快速反应和谨慎决策，而不仅仅是体力。 我并不认识他本人，所以我对他赛场外生活的印象来自采访和其他公开活动。他看起来冷静、自律，也很认真地追求进步。在场上，当对手带来新的难题时，他能够调整打法，这使他的比赛很有看头。 我钦佩的是他在压力下保持专注的能力。实力很强的选手同样会犯错，但必须马上调整，不能让上一分影响下一分。这一点对学习和研究也有启发。我不会把自己的工作直接与顶尖竞技运动相比，但他的耐心和坚持提供了一个有用的例子：进步需要的不只是短暂的热情。

关键词

Ma Long · table tennis · Olympic medals · calm · consistency

待确认素材与观点

  • 马龙的冠军成就是笼统公开背景；用户是否关注、看过采访、钦佩及具体感受均为候选；不写私人认识关系。

I visited the Golden Hall during my solo trip to Europe in June, when I also attended an academic conference. Before the trip, I came across it in an online guide to Vienna. I learned it was one of the city's famous cultural landmarks and wanted to include it while exploring the city. The building has an elegant, traditional appearance, with a sense of symmetry and decorative detail that feels quite different from a modern office block. What appeals to me is the atmosphere. The warm colours and careful design make it feel like a place created for people to pay attention and appreciate something together. Even focusing on the building itself gives me a connection to the city's musical culture. I stayed in Vienna longer than in Budapest, so Vienna became the main place I would recommend from that trip. The Golden Hall is part of the reason: it represents the cultural side of the city in a very concrete way. I study technology, but I still enjoy seeing how architecture and the arts shape a place's identity.

中文对照

关键词

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An athlete needs discipline, resilience, and the ability to respond calmly under pressure. Team sports also require communication and respect for others. Physical ability is important, but training intelligently and recovering properly matter too. I would add honesty and good sportsmanship, because how someone behaves when they lose can say as much about them as winning does.

原题与完整提示

Should students have physical education and do sports at school?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Yes, physical education gives students a chance to move and learn skills they may not discover at home. Sport can also teach cooperation and help break up long periods of sitting. Classes should include different activities and suit different abilities. The aim should be healthy participation, not making students feel embarrassed because they aren't the fastest or strongest.

中文对照

是的，体育课让学生活动、学习在家未必发现的技能。运动还能教合作、打断久坐。课程应有不同活动、适配不同能力。目标应是健康参与，而非让学生因不是最快最强而尴尬。

喜欢的公共建筑

原题与完整提示

Why do people visit historic buildings?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Historic buildings give people a physical connection to the past. Their design and materials can reveal how earlier generations lived or what they valued. Some visitors also enjoy the beauty and atmosphere. Reading about a place is useful, but standing in the actual space can make its history feel more concrete and encourage questions that a textbook might not raise.

中文对照

历史建筑让人与过去产生实物联系。设计材料能透露前人如何生活、重视什么。有些访客也享受美与氛围。阅读地点资料有用，但站在真实空间，让历史更具体，也引出教科书未必提出的问题。

关键词

past · design · atmosphere · physical connection
