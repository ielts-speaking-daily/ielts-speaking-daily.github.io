拥有成功商业的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person you know who has a successful business You should say: Who this person is How you got to know him/her Why and how he/she started the business What business he/she does And explain why you think the business is successful

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Someone I know with a successful business is a family acquaintance who runs a small computer repair and support shop. I got to know them when they helped someone in my family with a device problem. They started the business because they had technical experience and noticed that local customers needed help they could understand. Instead of focusing only on selling new equipment, the shop deals with repairs, setup problems, and basic advice about using computers safely. At the beginning, they kept the service range fairly small and relied on careful work and recommendations. What stands out is their way of explaining a problem. They tell customers what is necessary, what can wait, and whether repairing a device is actually worthwhile. That makes people feel less worried about being charged for something they don't need. I think the business is successful because customers return and recommend it to others. Success here isn't about becoming a huge company immediately. It is about earning a stable reputation and solving everyday problems reliably. As someone interested in computing, I appreciate the combination of technical knowledge and communication. A repair is more useful when the customer also understands how to avoid the same issue again.

中文对照

我认识的一位生意做得很成功的人，是家里的一位熟人，对方经营一家小型电脑维修与技术支持店。当时家人遇到了设备问题，得到了对方的帮助，我也因此认识了这位店主。 对方开店，是因为有技术经验，也注意到本地顾客需要能够听懂的帮助。店里并不只专注于卖新设备，也会处理维修、设置问题，并提供安全使用电脑的基础建议。 刚开始时，对方把服务范围控制得比较小，主要依靠认真做事和他人推荐。最突出的地方，是解释问题的方式。对方会告诉顾客哪些处理是必要的、哪些可以等等，以及这台设备到底值不值得修。这让顾客不必那么担心被收取不必要的费用。 我认为这门生意成功，是因为顾客会回来，也会推荐给别人。这里的成功并不是马上变成一家大公司，而是建立稳定的口碑，可靠地解决日常问题。我对计算机感兴趣，因此很欣赏技术知识与沟通能力的结合。如果顾客也能理解怎样避免同样的问题再次发生，那么这次维修就更有价值。

关键词

repair shop · technical experience · clear explanations · trust · returning customers

待确认素材与观点

  • 经营者及认识方式、开店经过、服务做法、回头客和成功评价均为完整候选；不写真实收入或已核验商业成果。

近期改变的计划

原题与完整提示

Describe a plan that you had to change recently You should say: When this happened What made you change the plan What the new plan was And how you felt about the change

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A plan I had to change was a weekend visit to a park with a friend. We had arranged it a few weeks ago because we both wanted a break from studying and thought a long walk would be relaxing. On the morning of the visit, the weather became very rainy. We could have continued, but walking around outside no longer seemed enjoyable or convenient. We talked briefly and decided to choose an indoor activity instead of cancel everything. The new plan was to meet at a library and then have something to eat nearby. We each looked for a book and spent some quiet time reading before talking over a meal. It was a different kind of break, but still gave us time away from our normal tasks. At first, I was disappointed because I had been looking forward to fresh air. Once we changed the plan, I felt better. I like reading, and I'm more talkative with familiar people, so the alternative suited me quite well. The experience reminded me that the purpose of a plan can matter more than its exact details. We wanted to relax together, and that was still possible.

中文对照

我曾经改过一个计划：周末和朋友去公园。几周前，我们约好了这次出行，因为都想从学习中暂时放松一下，觉得走一段长路会很舒服。 出行当天早上下起了大雨。我们本来可以照常出发，但在户外散步已经不太愉快，也不方便。简单商量之后，我们决定改成室内活动，而不是取消整个计划。 新的安排是在图书馆见面，然后去附近吃点东西。我们各自找了一本书，安静地读了一会儿，再边吃饭边聊天。虽然休息的方式变了，我们仍然得以暂时放下平时的任务。 起初我有些失望，因为一直期待出去呼吸新鲜空气。调整安排后，我的心情好了些。我喜欢阅读，而且在熟悉的人面前比较健谈，所以这个替代方案也很适合我。这次经历让我意识到，计划的目的有时比具体细节更重要。我们想一起放松一下，而这个目的仍然实现了。

关键词

weekend park · rain · library · reading · same purpose

待确认素材与观点

  • 周末朋友计划、几周前、雨天、图书馆与用餐经过及感受为授权候选；不声称真实天气或行程。

Why do you think parents still make plans for their children nowadays?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Parents often want to protect their children from mistakes and help them prepare for the future. They may also feel that their own experience gives them a useful perspective. However, planning everything can prevent children from becoming independent. I think parents should discuss options and explain risks, while gradually allowing children to make age-appropriate choices themselves.
