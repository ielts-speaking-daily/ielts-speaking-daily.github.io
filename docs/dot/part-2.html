对结果开心的重要决定

原题与完整提示

Describe a time you made an important decision and were happy with the result You should say: What the decision was Why you made the decision How easy it was for you to make the decision And explain why you were happy with the result

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An important decision I was happy with was choosing to focus on robotics during my master's studies in artificial intelligence. I already enjoyed programming, but I wanted to work on something that could interact with the real world. It wasn't an instant decision. I spent some time comparing research topics and discussing them with my supervisor. Robotics seemed challenging because it involved more than making a program work on a screen. A robot also has to deal with a changing environment. Eventually, I decided that the challenge was part of the attraction. I could build on my interest in computers while learning something new. Looking back, the decision gave my studies a clearer direction. I'm happy with the result because it connects with my next goal: doing a PhD abroad and researching embodied AI. I don't expect every experiment to succeed, but I feel that I'm moving towards a field I want to understand more deeply. For me, that sense of direction is more valuable than choosing the easiest option.

一个让我对结果满意的重要决定，是在人工智能硕士阶段选择重点研究机器人。我本来就喜欢编程，但也想研究能与现实世界互动的东西。 这并不是瞬间作出的决定。我花了一些时间比较研究主题，并与导师讨论。机器人似乎很有挑战性，因为它不只是让程序在屏幕上运行。机器人还必须应对不断变化的环境。 最终，我认为这种挑战本身就有吸引力。我既能利用对计算机的兴趣，又能学习新东西。回头看，这个决定让我的学习方向更清晰。 我对结果满意，因为它与下一步目标相连：赴海外读博并研究具身智能。我不期待每次实验都成功，但感觉自己正在走向一个想深入理解的领域。对我而言，这种方向感比选择最容易的选项更有价值。

做困难事情并成功的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person you know who did something difficult and was successful You should say: Who this person is What difficult thing this person did Why this person was successful And explain how you feel about this person

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

I'd like to talk about my supervisor. They managed to get a difficult robotics project working after several unsuccessful attempts. The problem was that the system performed well in a simple test, but became unreliable when the conditions changed. Rather than changing everything at once, my supervisor broke the problem into smaller parts. They checked the data, compared the results and asked the team to keep a clear record of each experiment. That made it easier to see what was actually causing the failure. The project eventually worked because they were patient and systematic. They also listened to other people's suggestions instead of assuming that their first idea must be correct. I think that combination was more useful than simply working longer hours. I admire them because the experience showed me what good research can look like. I'm studying AI and robotics myself, so I find this kind of example especially relevant. It reminds me that success often comes from understanding a problem properly and making steady improvements, rather than finding a quick trick.

中文对照

我想讲讲我的导师。导师经过几次不成功的尝试，最终让一个困难的机器人项目运行起来。问题是，系统在简单测试中表现良好，但条件变化后就变得不可靠。 导师没有一下子改动所有部分，而是把问题拆分开来，逐一检查数据、比较结果，并让团队清楚地记录每次实验。这样更容易发现究竟是什么导致失败。 项目最终成功，是因为导师有耐心、有条理。导师也会听取别人的建议，而不是认定自己的第一个想法一定正确。我认为，这些品质结合起来，比单纯延长工作时间更有帮助。 我很敬佩导师，因为这次经历让我看到了优秀的研究工作可以是什么样。我自己也学习人工智能和机器人，因此这个例子对我尤其有启发。它提醒我，成功往往来自真正理解问题并持续改进，而不是找到一个快速窍门。

关键词

supervisor · unreliable robot · small steps · experiment records · steady improvement

待确认素材与观点

  • 具体机器人项目、失败与成功过程均为候选；导师的这些特点需确认

She smiles, makes little comments and keeps the conversation going. What I find appealing is that her happiness feels fairly steady. It doesn't mean she never has worries. A very organized person I'd like to talk about is my supervisor. They are a good person, and what stands out to me is how they manage research work, rather than just how tidy their desk looks. 关键词
