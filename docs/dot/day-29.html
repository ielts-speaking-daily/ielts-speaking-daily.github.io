改变重要想法

原题与完整提示

Describe a time when you changed an important opinion of yours You should say: When you changed your opinion What the original opinion was Why you changed it And explain how you felt about the experience

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An important opinion I changed was the idea that an impressive demonstration is enough to show that an AI system is useful. Earlier this year, while thinking more carefully about robotics, I began to see the limits of that view. Originally, I was strongly attracted to a system that could complete a task in a short video. If the result looked smooth, it was easy to assume that the underlying technology was ready for practical use. But a demonstration may show only the best attempt under carefully selected conditions. What changed my mind was considering how the same system would behave when the situation was slightly different. A robot might face an unfamiliar object, poor lighting or a person moving unexpectedly. Those conditions reveal whether it can actually cope, rather than simply repeat a successful example. I now think reliability, testing and clear limits matter as much as an attractive demonstration. I felt that the new view was less exciting at first, but more realistic. It did not make me less interested in AI. Instead, it made the questions I wanted to ask more useful: not just whether something can work once, but whether people can depend on it.

中文对照

我改变的一个重要观点，是认为令人印象深刻的演示足以证明AI有用。今年较早时，更认真思考机器人后，我开始看到这种想法的局限。 原先，我很容易被短视频中成功完成任务的系统吸引。如果结果流畅，就容易以为技术已可实际使用。但演示可能只展示精心挑选的条件下表现最好的一次尝试。 改变想法的是思考同一系统在情境略有变化时会怎样。机器人可能遇到陌生物体、光线不好，或有人突然移动。这些情况才说明它能否应对，而非只重复成功例子。 现在我认为可靠性、测试和明确局限，与漂亮演示同样重要。起初新观点没那么令人兴奋，却更现实，也没让我失去AI兴趣，反而让问题更有用：不只看能否成功一次，而是看人们能否依赖它。

关键词

an impressive demonstration · selected conditions · unfamiliar situations · reliability and testing · depend on it

待确认素材与观点

  • 旧观点、观点改变时间和思考过程为候选成长故事，尚未确认，不作为用户真实研究记录。

想要颁布的环保法律

原题与完整提示

Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce You should say: What law it should be Why people should follow the law Whether the law will be popular And explain how you feel about this law

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An environmental law I would like to see introduced is a requirement for electronic products to carry clear information about how repairable they are. Manufacturers would need to explain whether common parts can be replaced and how long suitable spare parts will be available. The reason is that a small fault can sometimes lead people to replace an entire device. If they knew a repair was possible and affordable, they might keep it longer. That could reduce waste and the resources needed to produce a new product. Shops and manufacturers would need to follow the rule so consumers could compare information fairly. I think the law could be popular with people who want to avoid unnecessary spending. Some companies might object because providing parts and clear information takes effort. So the requirement would need a practical timetable and consistent standards rather than a vague promise. I would feel positive about it because it connects environmental protection with an everyday decision. It would not force everyone to keep a broken device forever, but it would give them a realistic choice before buying something new. That seems more helpful than simply telling people to consume less.

中文对照

我希望推出一条环保法规，要求电子产品清楚标明可维修程度。制造商需要说明常见零件能否更换，以及合适的备件能供应多久。 原因是，一个小故障有时就会让人更换整台设备。如果知道能够维修，而且费用合理，人们就可能使用更久，从而减少废弃物，以及生产新产品所需的资源。商店和制造商都需要遵守这条规定，让消费者能够公平地比较这些信息。 我认为，希望避免不必要支出的人会欢迎这条法规。不过，有些公司可能会反对，因为供应配件和提供清楚的信息都需要投入。因此，这项要求需要切实可行的时间表和统一的标准，而不是一个模糊的承诺。 我会支持这条法规，因为它把环保与日常生活中的决定联系起来。它不会强迫每个人永远留着坏掉的设备，而是在购买新产品前，给人们一个可行的选择。这似乎比单纯劝人少消费更有帮助。

关键词

repairability information · replace common parts · reduce waste · consistent standards · a realistic choice
