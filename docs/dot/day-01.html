I spent some time comparing research topics and discussing them with my supervisor. Robotics seemed challenging because it involved more than making a program work on a screen. A robot also has to deal with a changing environment. Eventually, I decided that the challenge was part of the attraction. I could build on my interest in computers while learning something new. Looking back, the decision gave my studies a clearer direction. I'm happy with the result because it connects with my next goal: doing a PhD abroad and researching embodied AI. I don't expect every experiment to succeed, but I feel that I'm moving towards a field I want to understand more deeply. For me, that sense of direction is more valuable than choosing the easiest option.

原题与完整提示

Describe a person you know who did something difficult and was successful You should say: Who this person is What difficult thing this person did Why this person was successful And explain how you feel about this person

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

I'd like to talk about my supervisor. They managed to get a difficult robotics project working after several unsuccessful attempts. The problem was that the system performed well in a simple test, but became unreliable when the conditions changed. Rather than changing everything at once, my supervisor broke the problem into smaller parts. They checked the data, compared the results and asked the team to keep a clear record of each experiment. That made it easier to see what was actually causing the failure. The project eventually worked because they were patient and systematic. They also listened to other people's suggestions instead of assuming that their first idea must be correct. I think that combination was more useful than simply working longer hours. I admire them because the experience showed me what good research can look like. I'm studying AI and robotics myself, so I find this kind of example especially relevant. It reminds me that success often comes from understanding a problem properly and making steady improvements, rather than finding a quick trick.

中文对照

我想讲讲我的导师。导师经过几次不成功的尝试，最终让一个困难的机器人项目运行起来。问题是，系统在简单测试中表现良好，但条件变化后就变得不可靠。 导师没有一下子改动所有部分，而是把问题拆分开来，逐一检查数据、比较结果，并让团队清楚地记录每次实验。这样更容易发现究竟是什么导致失败。 项目最终成功，是因为导师有耐心、有条理。导师也会听取别人的建议，而不是认定自己的第一个想法一定正确。我认为，这些品质结合起来，比单纯延长工作时间更有帮助。 我很敬佩导师，因为这次经历让我看到了优秀的研究工作可以是什么样。我自己也学习人工智能和机器人，因此这个例子对我尤其有启发。它提醒我，成功往往来自真正理解问题并持续改进，而不是找到一个快速窍门。

关键词

supervisor · unreliable robot · small steps · experiment records · steady improvement

待确认素材与观点

  • 具体机器人项目、失败与成功过程均为候选；导师的这些特点需确认

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Money is one sign of success, but it isn't the whole picture. Someone may earn a lot while feeling exhausted or having little time for their family. Another person may earn less but do meaningful work and keep learning. I would judge success through a mixture of financial security, personal progress, and satisfaction with daily life.

How can companies help protect the environment?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Companies can reduce waste in both production and packaging. They can also use energy more efficiently and make products that last longer. For a technology company, repairing devices rather than immediately replacing them could help. These changes work best when they become normal business practice, rather than a temporary campaign used only to attract attention.

中文对照

公司可以减少生产和包装中的浪费，也能提高能源使用效率，让产品更耐用。对于科技公司，修理设备而不是马上更换可能有帮助。当这些改变成为日常经营做法，而非只为吸引注意的临时活动时，效果最好。

关键词

reduce waste · energy · repair · lasting change

Is it difficult to do business in today's world?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

It can be difficult because customers have many choices and businesses face fast changes. A small company may compete with firms that have much bigger budgets. At the same time, online tools make it easier to reach customers and test ideas. So the environment is challenging, but people also have useful resources that weren't available before.

中文对照

做生意可能很难，因为顾客选择多，企业面对快速变化。小公司可能要与预算大得多的企业竞争。同时，网络工具让接触顾客和测试想法更容易。所以环境有挑战，但人们也拥有过去没有的有用资源。
