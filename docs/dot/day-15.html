读起来开心的书

原题与完整提示

Describe a book you enjoyed reading and that made you happy You should say: What kind of book it was What it was about Why you read it And explain why it made you happy

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A book I enjoyed reading is The Little Prince. It is a short imaginative story that looks simple at first, but raises questions about friendship, responsibility, and how adults see the world. The story follows a young prince who leaves his small home and visits different places. Along the way, he meets characters whose lives are shaped by things such as power or numbers. His relationships with a rose and a fox give the story a more personal and emotional side. I chose it because I wanted something different from technical reading. As an AI student, I spend time thinking about systems and practical problems, so a gentle story offered a change of pace. I could read a little without feeling that I had to memorize every detail. It made me happy because it encouraged me to pay attention to simple relationships rather than only measurable achievements. I also liked the contrast between the child's questions and the adults' complicated habits. The book didn't solve a particular problem, but it left me calmer and reminded me that curiosity and care are valuable. That's the kind of feeling I want from leisure reading.

中文对照

我喜欢读的一本书是《小王子》。这是一部充满想象力的短篇故事，乍看很简单，却提出了关于友谊、责任以及成年人如何看待世界的问题。 故事围绕一位离开自己小小家园、前往不同地方的年轻王子展开。一路上，他遇见了生活被权力、数字等事物主导的人物。他与玫瑰和狐狸之间的关系，也让故事更有个人情感色彩。 我选择这本书，是因为想读一些与技术材料不同的内容。作为人工智能专业的学生，我会花时间思考系统和实际问题，因此这样一个温和的故事能让我换一种节奏。我可以读上一小段，而不用觉得自己必须记住每一个细节。 它让我开心，因为它鼓励我关注简单的人际关系，而不只是可以衡量的成就。我也喜欢孩子的提问与成年人复杂习惯之间的对比。这本书没有解决某个具体问题，但它让我更平静，也提醒我好奇心和关怀很有价值。这正是我希望从休闲阅读中获得的感受。

关键词

The Little Prince · friendship · rose and fox · technical reading · curiosity

待确认素材与观点

  • 《小王子》为用户授权的书籍候选；真实读过、阅读动机和幸福感尚待确认。情节为作品一般背景，不登记为用户阅读完成。

帮助他人

原题与完整提示

Describe a time when you helped someone You should say： Where you helped him/her Why you helped How you helped And explain how you felt about it

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A time I helped someone was when a classmate was struggling with a small programming task at university. We were in a study area, and they had a program that seemed to run but didn't produce the expected result. I offered to help because I knew how frustrating that situation could be. At first, I didn't change the code. I asked them to explain what they wanted it to do and what they had already checked. Then we reduced the task to a simpler example and looked at the output step by step. Eventually, we noticed that one part of the program was using the wrong input. After fixing that, we tested it again and checked that the result matched the goal. I also explained why the smaller example had made the problem easier to locate. I felt pleased, mainly because my classmate could continue independently afterwards. I enjoy programming, but helping someone understand a process is different from solving everything for them. It was a fairly ordinary event, yet it showed how useful patient questions can be. I also learned to explain my own thinking more clearly instead of assuming that the other person saw the problem in the same way.

中文对照

我帮助别人的一次经历，是大学里一位同学做小型编程任务时遇到了困难。当时我们在一个学习区，对方的程序似乎可以运行，却没有产生预期的结果。 我主动提出帮忙，因为我知道这种情况会有多令人沮丧。一开始，我没有改代码，而是请对方解释希望程序完成什么，以及已经检查过哪些地方。然后，我们把任务简化成一个更小的例子，一步步查看输出结果。 最后，我们发现程序中的一个部分使用了错误的输入。修正后，我们重新测试，确认结果符合目标。我也解释了为什么这个更小的例子能让问题更容易定位。 我感到很高兴，主要是因为之后同学可以独立继续做下去。我喜欢编程，但帮助别人理解一个过程，与替对方解决所有问题是不同的。虽然这是一次很普通的经历，却让我看到耐心提问有多有用。我也学会了更清楚地解释自己的思路，而不是假定别人看待问题的方式与我相同。

关键词

classmate · programming · simple example · wrong input · patient questions

待确认素材与观点

  • 同学、地点、编程帮助事件、错误原因和感受全部为授权候选；不可作为已发生助人证据。

原题与完整提示

How can parents help children develop a love of reading?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Parents can read with children and let them choose books that match their interests. Keeping books within easy reach makes reading a normal option. Talking about a story is often more encouraging than testing every detail. Adults should show their own enjoyment too. The aim is a pleasant habit, rather than turning every book into extra homework or a competition.

中文对照

家长可共读，并让孩子选符合兴趣的书。书放在容易拿到的地方，让阅读成为普通选择。谈故事常比考每个细节更鼓励。成年人也应展示阅读乐趣。目标是愉快习惯，而非把每本书变额外作业或竞赛。

关键词

read together · choice · access · enjoyment

帮助他人

原题与完整提示

Should children be taught to be kind to others?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Yes, kindness helps children build relationships and notice how their actions affect others. Parents and teachers can model it through listening, sharing, and respectful language. Specific examples are more useful than simply saying, 'Be nice.' Children should also understand that kindness doesn't mean accepting harmful behaviour or giving up every personal boundary. Respect should apply to themselves as well as other people.

中文对照

是的，友善帮助孩子建立关系、注意行为如何影响别人。家长老师可通过倾听、分享、尊重语言示范。具体例子比只说“友好点”有用。孩子也应理解友善不等于接受伤害行为或放弃所有边界。尊重同时适用于自己和他人。

关键词

帮助他人

原题与完整提示

Should people be very kind when they help others?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Help is more valuable when it is offered respectfully, because the person receiving it may already feel vulnerable. A patient explanation can make practical assistance easier to accept. However, kindness doesn't mean agreeing to every request or promising things we can't do. Honest limits and attention to what the person actually needs are part of helping well.

中文对照

尊重地帮助更有价值，因为接受者可能已脆弱。耐心解释使实际帮助更易接受。但友善不等于答应所有请求或承诺做不到的事。诚实边界、关注真实需要，也是好帮助的一部分。

关键词

respect · patience · honest limits · actual needs
