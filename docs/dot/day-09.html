浪费时间的活动

原题与完整提示

Describe an activity you do regularly that you think is a waste of time You should say: What it is When you usually do it Why you do it And explain why you think it is a waste of time

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An activity I'd describe as a waste of time is scrolling through short videos without choosing what I actually want to watch. It would usually happen when I felt tired after studying and wanted a quick break. I might open an app intending to watch for a few minutes. The problem is that each video is short, so it's easy to keep going without noticing how much time has passed. Afterwards, I may not remember much of what I watched. I would do it because it doesn't require much effort. Compared with reading a book or working on a program, it's a very easy way to fill a quiet moment. But that doesn't always mean it helps me rest properly. I think it wastes time when I lose control of the length of the break. I don't mean that all entertainment is pointless; I enjoy reading and anime when I choose them deliberately. The difference is whether I'm spending time on something I value or simply following the next suggestion. I'd like to set a limit and choose my breaks more consciously.

中文对照

我会把没有明确想看什么、一直刷短视频描述为浪费时间的活动。它通常发生在学习后疲倦、想短暂休息时。 我可能原本只打算打开应用看几分钟。问题是每段视频都很短，因此很容易一直看，不察觉时间过了多久。看完后，可能记不住太多内容。 我会这么做，是因为它不太费力。与读书或编程相比，刷视频是很容易打发空闲时间的一种方式，但这不代表能真正休息好。 当我无法控制休息长度时，就觉得浪费了时间。我不是说所有娱乐都没有意义；主动选择读书和看动漫时，我会享受它们。区别在于，我是否把时间用于自己重视的东西，还是只追随下一个推荐。我希望设置限制，更有意识地选择休息方式。

关键词

short videos · quick break · lose track of time · deliberate leisure · set a limit

待确认素材与观点

  • 刷短视频的习惯、频率和失控体验为候选，不把动漫兴趣自动归为浪费时间

拥挤地方

原题与完整提示

Describe a crowded place you went to You should say: Where it was When you went there Who you went there with What you did there

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A crowded place I'd like to describe is a railway station in Vienna during my European trip in June. I was travelling on my own, so I went there alone and needed to find the right place to wait for my train. The station was particularly busy because several trains were due to leave around the same time. People were checking the displays, moving with suitcases and waiting near the platforms. It took a little more attention than usual to get through the crowd. I checked the departure information, found somewhere to stand and made sure my luggage stayed close to me. I wasn't doing anything very unusual, but a simple task felt more demanding when so many people were moving around. The experience was memorable because it was part of travelling abroad independently. I'm generally quiet, so I wouldn't choose a crowded place just for the excitement. Still, I understood that a station serves lots of people. Once I knew where I needed to go, I felt more comfortable. It showed me the value of allowing extra time rather than trying to arrive at the last minute.

中文对照

我想描述的一处拥挤地方，是六月欧洲旅行中的维也纳火车站。我独自旅行，因此也是一个人去车站，需要找到正确的候车位置。 车站特别忙，因为几趟列车差不多同时发车。人们查看显示屏、拖着行李移动、在站台附近等待。穿过人群比平常需要更多注意力。 我检查发车信息，找到候车的位置，并确保行李靠近自己。我没有做特别的事情，但当很多人移动时，简单任务也更费心。 这次经历让我印象深刻，因为它是独立出国旅行的一部分。我通常安静，不会专门为热闹而选择拥挤场所，不过我理解车站要服务很多人。知道该去哪后，我就更自在。这让我意识到，留出额外时间比最后一刻才赶到更有价值。

关键词

Vienna station · solo traveller · departure display · luggage · extra time

待确认素材与观点

  • 火车站城市、拥挤场面、具体行动和感受均是欧洲行候选扩展；真实等车聊天的城市仍未知

原题与完整提示

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A person who learns history and really enjoys it is a friend I met through my studies. We became close partly because we both liked reading, although we were interested in different kinds of books. They learn history by reading books, visiting museums and watching carefully chosen documentaries. What I find interesting is that they compare different accounts instead of accepting the first version they hear. They also like asking what daily life was like for ordinary people, not just remembering famous dates. They enjoy history because it helps them understand why places and societies became the way they are. A building or a painting means more to them when they know something about the period in which it was made. I respect this interest because it requires curiosity and patience. During my own European trip, I visited museums in Vienna, so I can see how learning some background could make a visit more meaningful. I'm more focused on AI and robotics, but I like talking to someone whose interests encourage me to look beyond my own subject.

中文对照

一位学习并喜欢历史的人，是我通过学习认识的朋友。我们变得熟悉，部分因为都喜欢阅读，虽然喜欢的书种类不同。 这位朋友通过读书、参观博物馆和观看经过挑选的纪录片学习历史。有趣的是，对方会比较不同叙述，而不是听到第一个版本就接受。也喜欢了解普通人的日常生活，而不只是记住著名日期。 对方喜欢历史，因为它帮助理解地方和社会为什么变成现在的样子。了解建筑或绘画创作的时代后，它们会更有意义。 我尊重这种兴趣，因为它需要好奇心和耐心。我自己欧洲旅行时参观了维也纳的博物馆，因此能理解一些背景知识会让参观更有意义。我更关注人工智能和机器人，但也喜欢和能让我超越本专业看问题的人交谈。

关键词

reading friend · books and museums · compare accounts · ordinary lives · broader interests

待确认素材与观点

  • 历史朋友、学习方式和交谈关系为候选；本人维也纳博物馆访问已确认
