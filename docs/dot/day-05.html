小时候学到的新技能

原题与完整提示

Describe a new skill you learned when you were a child You should say: What the skill was Who taught you this skill How you learned it And explain how you felt about learning the skill

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A skill I learned as a child was typing on a computer. I learned it during primary school with help from my mother. It was a small skill, but it gave me more independence when using a computer. At first, I had to look for each letter and used only a couple of fingers. My mother showed me how to place my hands and encouraged me to practise with short words rather than rushing straight into long passages. I improved by doing a little practice regularly. I would type a few sentences, check the mistakes and try again. It was easier to notice progress when I compared my work with something I'd typed before. I felt proud when typing became more natural. It meant I could write and look things up with less help. Looking back, that skill fits well with my current interests, because I now enjoy programming and spending time on computers. It shows how a simple childhood skill can become the starting point for a much bigger interest, even if the early practice wasn't particularly exciting.

中文对照

可以描述的一项童年技能是电脑打字。我在小学阶段由母亲帮助学习。虽然是小技能，却让我使用电脑时更独立。 开始时，我必须寻找每个字母，而且只用一两根手指。母亲教我如何摆放双手，并鼓励我先练短单词，而不是急着输入长文章。 我通过定期进行一点练习来进步。我会输入几句话，检查错误，再重试。与以前打过的内容比较时，更容易发现进步。 打字变得自然时，我很自豪。这意味着我能更少依靠别人来写东西和搜索信息。回头看，这项技能很契合我现在的兴趣，因为现在我喜欢编程和使用电脑。它能说明，一个简单的童年技能如何成为更大兴趣的起点，即使初期练习并不特别有趣。

关键词

typing · primary school · mother · short practice · independence

待确认素材与观点

  • 童年学习打字、母亲教授、练习过程全部为候选；确认或替换后作为个人经历使用

university friend · ordinary places · light and details · different perspective · shared outing
