快乐人士

原题与完整提示

Describe a happy person you know You should say: Who this person is What he/she is like How he/she shows happiness And explain why you think he/she is a happy person

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A happy person I'd like to describe is my mother. We live together in an apartment in Guangdong, so she is someone I know well. She is warm and able to enjoy small things in daily life. For example, she becomes cheerful when we have a relaxed meal or talk about something interesting. She doesn't need a big celebration to show that she's enjoying herself. She smiles, makes little comments and keeps the conversation going. What I find appealing is that her happiness feels fairly steady. It doesn't mean she never has worries. Instead, she tends to look for something manageable to do, rather than staying upset about everything at once. I'm usually quiet and quite rational, but I can be talkative with people I'm close to. That makes our conversations a useful way for me to relax. I think she's a happy person because she notices everyday pleasures and makes people around her feel at ease. It's a kind of happiness that's simple and easy to share.

中文对照

我想描述的一位快乐的人是我的母亲。我们一起住在广东的一套公寓里，因此她是我很熟悉的人。她待人温暖，也能从日常小事中获得快乐。 例如，我们轻松吃顿饭或聊到有趣的事时，她会变得很开心。她不需要盛大的庆祝来表现自己享受生活。她会微笑，发表几句自己的看法，让聊天自然地继续下去。 我很欣赏的一点是，她的快乐比较稳定。这不意味着她从不担忧。她会寻找自己能处理的事情，而不是一下子对所有事情都感到烦恼。 我平常安静、比较理性，但在亲近的人面前会比较健谈。因此，我们的交谈是我放松的一种方式。我认为她是快乐的人，因为她会注意日常的小乐趣，也让周围的人感到自在。这种快乐简单，也容易分享。

关键词

mother · small pleasures · relaxed conversations · steady happiness · feel at ease

待确认素材与观点

  • 母亲的性格、表现快乐的具体方式和日常交谈场景待确认；同住事实已确认

看过的名人采访

原题与完整提示

Describe a time you watched a famous person being interviewed You should say: Who the famous person was What the interview was about When and where you watched the interview And explain how you felt about the interview

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

I'd like to describe an online interview with Fei-Fei Li, who is well known for her work in artificial intelligence. I watched it on my computer at home a few months ago, while looking for something related to my studies. The interview was about AI, its possibilities and the importance of thinking about people when developing technology. I was interested because my master's research is related to robotics, and I want to continue studying embodied AI in the future. What appealed to me was the connection between technical work and real-world needs. It's easy to become focused on whether a system achieves a good result in a test. But we also need to consider whether it is useful, reliable and suitable for the people who will use it. I found the interview encouraging because it gave me a broader way to think about my subject. It also reminded me that a researcher needs to explain ideas clearly, not just understand the technical details. I'd like to watch more conversations like this because they make academic work feel connected to wider issues.

中文对照

我想描述一段与李飞飞有关的网络采访，她因人工智能领域的工作而知名。几个月前，我在家里的电脑上观看了采访，当时是在找与专业相关的内容。 采访讨论人工智能、它的可能性，以及开发技术时考虑人的重要性。我感兴趣，是因为我的硕士研究与机器人相关，也希望未来继续研究具身智能。 吸引我的是技术工作与现实需求之间的联系。我们很容易只关注系统在测试中是否取得好结果，但也需要考虑它是否有用、可靠，是否适合未来使用它的人。 这段采访让我受到鼓励，因为它让我以更广的视角思考专业。它也提醒我，研究者不仅要理解技术细节，还要清楚解释想法。我想多看这样的对谈，因为它们能让学术工作与更广泛的问题产生联系。

关键词

Fei-Fei Li · online interview · AI and people · real-world needs · clear explanations

待确认素材与观点

  • 观看约几个月前为候选时间；是否看过此类真实采访及实际视频内容待确认，未核对某一采访链接，未编造直接引语
