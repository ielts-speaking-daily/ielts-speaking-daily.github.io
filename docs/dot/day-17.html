去过的无聊地方

原题与完整提示

Describe a boring place You should say: Where it is Who you went there with What you did there And explain why you think it is a boring place

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A place I found boring was a large furniture showroom in the city where I live. I went there with my mother one afternoon because we wanted to look at some options for the home. At first, I was interested in the different room designs. But after a while, many displays began to feel similar. We walked between rows of sofas, tables, and cupboards, checked labels, and discussed small differences in size and colour. The visit took longer than I had expected because there were many choices and it was difficult to narrow them down. I could see why those details mattered, but I didn't feel very involved in the process. There wasn't much to do except keep looking and comparing. I think the place was boring for me because the activity didn't connect strongly with my interests. I enjoy reading and programming, where I can follow an idea or solve a problem. In the showroom, the decisions felt repetitive and I had no clear task of my own. I wouldn't say it was a bad place; it was useful for its purpose. I would simply prefer a shorter visit with a clearer plan.

中文对照

我觉得无聊的一个地方，是我现在居住的城市里的一家大型家具展厅。某天下午，我和母亲一起去了那里，因为我们想看看有哪些适合家里的选择。 起初，我对不同的房间设计还有兴趣，但过了一会儿，很多展示开始让我觉得大同小异。我们在一排排沙发、桌子和柜子之间走动，查看标签，讨论尺寸和颜色上的细小差别。 这次逛展厅比我预期的时间更长，因为选择很多，很难缩小范围。我理解为什么那些细节很重要，但自己并没有很强的参与感。除了继续看、继续比较，也没有多少其他事情可做。 我觉得这个地方无聊，是因为这项活动与我的兴趣联系不大。我喜欢阅读和编程，因为可以顺着一个想法深入，或者解决一个问题。但在展厅里，做决定显得很重复，我自己也没有明确的任务。我不会说那里不好，它确实满足了自己的用途。我只是更希望参观时间短一些，事先的计划更明确一些。

关键词

furniture showroom · mother · repetitive choices · no clear task · shorter visit

待确认素材与观点

  • 家具购物、陪母亲、时间地点、具体行为和感受均为授权候选；母亲同住与兴趣为真，不扩写真实家装事实。

原题与完整提示

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A time I got up early was during my solo trip to Europe in June. I needed to catch a morning train, so I set an alarm for around six and prepared most of my things the night before. After waking up, I checked my documents, packed the remaining items, and made sure I had the information I needed for the journey. I then left early enough to find the correct platform without rushing. Because I was travelling alone, I had to keep track of those details myself. I got up early mainly to give myself a safety margin. In an unfamiliar place, finding the entrance or understanding a sign can take longer than expected. I thought it was better to have extra waiting time than feel anxious about missing the train. At first, I felt sleepy and would have preferred to stay in bed. Once everything was ready, though, I felt calmer. The experience made me appreciate preparation: an early start is much easier when there aren't lots of small decisions left to make. I wouldn't want to wake up that early every day, but for that journey it felt like a sensible choice.

中文对照

我早起的一次经历，是在六月独自去欧洲旅行的时候。当时我需要赶一班早上的火车，所以把闹钟设在大约六点，并在前一天晚上准备好了大部分东西。 起床后，我检查了证件，装好剩下的物品，并确认自己掌握了旅途中需要的信息。之后我提早出发，这样就有足够时间找到正确的站台，不用匆忙赶路。因为是独自旅行，这些细节都需要我自己留意。 我早起主要是为了给自己留出一些余量。在陌生的地方，找入口或看懂一个标识，都可能比预期花更长时间。我觉得，多等一会儿比担心错过火车更好。 起初，我有些困，更想继续躺在床上。不过，等一切准备好之后，我就平静多了。这次经历让我体会到提前准备的价值：当没有很多小决定留到最后再做时，早出发会容易得多。我并不想每天都那么早起，但对那次行程来说，这是个合理的选择。

关键词

Europe · morning train · six o'clock · prepare ahead · safety margin
