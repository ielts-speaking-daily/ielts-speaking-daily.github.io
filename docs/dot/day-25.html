英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A place I'd like to recommend is Vienna, the capital of Austria. I visited it during a solo trip to Europe in June. I went to Europe to attend an academic conference, and I spent more time in Vienna than in Budapest. While I was in Vienna, I visited the Golden Hall and some local museums. The city has a rich cultural atmosphere, and its distinctive architecture is one of the main things I'd recommend it for. If you enjoy museums and interesting buildings, there is plenty to explore. Trying the local food would also be a way to experience the city's culture and its distinctive flavours. Overall, I'd describe Vienna as a comfortable city to spend time in. For me, its appeal is the combination of culture, architecture and the overall experience of being there. One other memory from my European trip has stayed with me. While I was waiting for a train, I chatted with a man who was living on the streets, and I felt that people were very friendly. That conversation made the wider trip memorable as well. So Vienna would be my recommendation, especially for someone interested in culture and museums.
