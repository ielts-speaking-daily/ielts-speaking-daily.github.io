有条理的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person you know who is very organized You should say: Who this person is What this person usually does to stay organized In what situations you have noticed this quality And explain why you think being organized is important to him/her

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A very organized person I'd like to talk about is my supervisor. They are a good person, and what stands out to me is how they manage research work, rather than just how tidy their desk looks. They keep a short list of priorities and arrange regular discussions with students. Before a meeting, they ask us to write down the main problem, what we've tried and what kind of help we need. That stops the conversation from becoming too vague. I noticed this quality while preparing a robotics project. There were several things to check, and it was easy to jump between them without making progress. My supervisor helped us put the tasks in a sensible order and keep track of the results. Being organized matters to them because research involves uncertainty. A good plan can't guarantee success, but it can prevent unnecessary confusion. I admire this approach because I'm quite rational myself, and I prefer to understand what I'm doing and why. It's a habit I'd like to develop further.

中文对照

我想讲的一位很有条理的人是我的导师。导师人很好。让我印象深刻的是导师安排研究工作的方式，而不只是桌面是否整洁。 导师会保留一份简短的优先事项清单，并定期与学生讨论。开会前，导师会让我们写下主要问题、尝试过什么，以及需要什么帮助。这样，讨论就不会太笼统。 我在准备机器人项目时注意到了这种特质。当时有几件事需要检查，很容易在它们之间来回切换，却没有进展。导师帮助我们把任务按合理顺序排列，并记录结果。 有条理对导师很重要，因为研究包含不确定性。好的计划不能保证成功，但能避免不必要的混乱。我欣赏这种方式，因为我自己也比较理性，喜欢理解正在做什么以及为什么做。这也是我想进一步培养的习惯。

关键词

supervisor · priorities · regular meetings · task order · research uncertainty

待确认素材与观点

  • 导师安排会议、要求准备清单和指导项目等细节是候选

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A noisy place I can describe is a railway station during my European trip in June. I travelled to Europe on my own to attend an academic conference, and I also visited Vienna and Budapest. I was waiting for a train and trying to keep track of the departure information. There were announcements over the speakers, people talking and the sound of luggage being pulled across the floor. Trains arriving and leaving added another layer of noise. The place felt noisy because several different sounds were happening at the same time. It was harder to concentrate than it would have been in a quiet room. I would occasionally look at the display again to make sure I hadn't missed anything important. I don't think the noise was completely avoidable, because a station is a busy public place. Still, clear announcements and a quieter waiting area would make it more comfortable. As someone who is usually fairly quiet, I'd prefer to have a place where I could sit and think without so many distractions.

中文对照

我可以描述一个嘈杂的地方，是六月欧洲旅行中的火车站。我独自赴欧洲参加学术会议，也参观了维也纳和布达佩斯。 我当时等火车，并试图留意发车信息。有广播、交谈声，以及行李箱在地面拖动的声音。列车到站和离站又增加了一层噪声。 这里显得嘈杂，是因为几种不同声音同时发生。相比安静的房间，我更难集中注意力。我会时不时再看显示屏，确认没有漏掉重要信息。 我认为这些声音不可能完全避免，因为车站是繁忙的公共场所。不过，清晰的广播和更安静的候车区会让体验更舒适。我通常比较安静，因此更希望有一个能坐下来思考、少受干扰的地方。

关键词

European trip · railway station · announcements · several sounds · quiet waiting area

原题与完整提示

Should children not be allowed to make noise under any circumstances?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

No, a complete ban would be unreasonable. Children make noise when they play, learn, or express excitement, and that is a normal part of growing up. They should learn to be quiet in places like libraries, though. Adults can explain the difference between settings and provide spaces where energetic play is welcome, instead of expecting silence everywhere.

中文对照

不，完全禁止不合理。孩子玩耍、学习或表达兴奋时会发出声音，这是成长的正常部分。但应学会在图书馆等地方保持安静。成年人可以解释不同场景，并提供欢迎活跃玩耍的空间，而非要求处处无声。

原题与完整提示

Do you think it is good for children to make noise?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Making some noise can be positive when children are playing together or asking questions. It can show confidence and involvement rather than bad behaviour. But volume still matters, especially when other people need rest. I would encourage children to express themselves while teaching them to notice their surroundings and respect situations that call for quiet.

中文对照

孩子一起玩或提问时发出一些声音可以是积极的，体现自信与参与，而非行为不好。但音量仍然重要，尤其当其他人需要休息。我会鼓励孩子表达，同时教他们留意周围环境、尊重需要安静的场合。

关键词
