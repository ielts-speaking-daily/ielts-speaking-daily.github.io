教你新技能的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person who taught you a new skill You should say: Who this person was What the skill was How you learned it And explain how you felt about this person

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A person who taught me a useful new skill is my supervisor. The skill I'd describe is debugging a program in a more systematic way, which is particularly relevant to my studies in AI and robotics. Before learning it, I would sometimes change several things at once when a program didn't work. That made it difficult to know which change had helped or created another problem. My supervisor showed me how to reduce the problem to a smaller example and check one possibility at a time. I learned by applying the method to a real task and explaining each step during our discussion. Instead of giving me the answer immediately, they asked questions that helped me see what evidence I had and what I was only assuming. I felt grateful because the skill was useful beyond that one piece of code. It gave me a calmer way to approach unfamiliar problems. I'm quite rational, so I liked having a method I could understand and reuse. I also appreciated being treated as someone who could learn to solve the problem independently.

中文对照

一位教会我实用新技能的人是我的导师。我想描述的技能，是更有条理地调试程序，它与人工智能和机器人学习尤其相关。 学会之前，程序运行出问题时，我有时会一次修改几个地方。这使我很难判断哪项修改起了作用，又是哪项引出了新问题。导师教我把问题缩小到更简单的例子，一次检查一种可能性。 我通过把方法用于具体任务，并在讨论时解释每一步来学习。导师没有马上给答案，而是提问，让我区分手里的证据与仅仅作出的假设。 我感到感激，因为这项技能不只对那段代码有用，也让我更冷静地处理陌生问题。我比较理性，因此喜欢能理解并重复使用的方法。我也很欣赏导师把我当成能学会独立解决问题的人。

关键词

supervisor · systematic debugging · small example · one change · independent problem-solving

待确认素材与观点

  • 导师教过调试、具体错误和指导过程均为候选；不要登记为已发生或已掌握的技能

A film I didn't like was one of the Transformers films. I watched it at home on my computer during a weekend, because the idea of robots was connected with an interest I already had. The film was mainly about large robots and conflicts between different groups. There were a lot of action scenes and impressive visual effects. Those parts looked exciting, but they weren't enough to keep me interested throughout the whole story. What I didn't like was that the action sometimes seemed more important than the characters' reasons for doing things. Do you think people should learn ancient history before learning modern history?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

background · modern interest · roots · timeline

Why is self-discipline important when learning a new skill?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A new skill usually requires repeated practice after the first excitement has faded. Self-discipline helps people continue when progress is slow or mistakes become frustrating.
