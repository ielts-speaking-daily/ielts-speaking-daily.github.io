特别场合的食物

原题与完整提示

Describe a food that people eat on special occasions/events You should say: What it is What the special event/occasion is How it is cooked/made And explain why people eat it on that special occasion/event

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

I'd like to talk about dumplings, which are associated with the Spring Festival in many families in China, especially in the north. They are small parcels of filling wrapped in thin pieces of dough, and they can be served as part of a family meal. To make them, people prepare a filling, often with vegetables and meat, although there are many different versions. A small amount is placed in the centre of a wrapper, and the edges are pressed together carefully so the filling stays inside. The dumplings can then be boiled, steamed or cooked in a pan, depending on the style. One reason they suit a special occasion is that making them can be a shared activity. Several people can prepare the wrappers, fill them and talk at the same time. Dumplings are also associated with reunion and good wishes, so their meaning goes beyond their taste. I think they are a good example of how ordinary ingredients become part of a tradition. The food feels special because of the occasion and the people sharing it, rather than because it is especially expensive.

中文对照

我想谈饺子。在中国许多家庭，尤其北方家庭，饺子与春节联系紧密。它是用薄面皮包住馅料的小面食，可以成为家庭餐的一部分。 制作时先准备馅料，通常有蔬菜和肉，但也有很多不同版本。把少量馅放在面皮中央，仔细捏合边缘，避免馅漏出来。根据不同做法，之后可以煮、蒸或用平底锅烹制。 它适合特别场合的一个原因，是制作过程可以一起完成。几个人能够做面皮、包馅，同时聊天。饺子还与团聚和祝福关联，所以意义不只在味道。 我认为它很好地说明普通食材如何成为传统的一部分。食物之所以显得特别，是因为享用的场合和一起分享的人，而不是因为它特别昂贵。

关键词

dumplings · Spring Festival · filling and wrappers · a shared activity · reunion and good wishes

擅长学习和说语言的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person who is good at learning and speaking new languages You should say: How you got to know him/her How he/she learns a new language What languages he/she can speak And explain how you feel about him/her

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

The person I'd like to describe is a fellow student from my master's programme. I first got to know him during a discussion after a university seminar, when we discovered that we both enjoyed reading and Japanese animation. He speaks Chinese and English, and he can also communicate in Japanese. What impresses me is not simply the number of languages, but the way he keeps improving them. He reads short texts, listens to material he finds interesting and tries to use new expressions in conversation. Instead of memorising a long list and forgetting it, he returns to useful words in different situations. He is also comfortable asking someone to correct him. If he cannot remember a word, he explains the idea another way rather than ending the conversation. That makes his speaking feel natural, even when it is not perfect. I admire his patience and consistency. Learning a language can seem like an enormous task, but he turns it into small activities he can repeat. I would like to take that approach to my own English preparation, because regular use seems more practical than waiting until I feel completely ready.

中文对照

我想描述硕士项目里的一位同学。大学一次讲座后的讨论中，我们发现都喜欢阅读和日本动漫，于是认识了。 他会中文、英语，也能用日语交流。让我佩服的不是单纯会几种语言，而是持续进步的方式。他读短文、听感兴趣的材料，尝试在交流中使用新表达。他不是背完一长串单词就忘掉，而是在不同情境反复接触有用词语。 他也愿意请别人纠正。如果想不起某个词，会换种方式解释，而不是结束谈话。因此，即使不完美，表达也显得自然。 我欣赏他的耐心和持之以恒。学习一门语言可能看起来是项艰巨的任务，他却把它变成可重复的小活动。我也想把这种方法用于英语备考，因为经常实际使用英语，比等到自己觉得完全准备好更实际。

关键词

a fellow student · Chinese, English and Japanese · use new expressions · explain another way · patience and consistency

待确认素材与观点

  • 该同学、相识经过、语言能力和学习习惯均为候选故事，尚未确认。

原题与完整提示

What are the differences between everyday food and festival food?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Everyday food is usually chosen for convenience, nutrition and cost. Festival food may involve more preparation, a particular tradition or dishes that are shared with a larger group. It can feel special because people do not eat it so often. However, the distinction is not absolute; a familiar simple dish can also become meaningful on a celebration day.

原题与完整提示

Are there any differences between the food people eat today and the food people ate in the past?

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

Many people now have access to a wider range of ingredients and meals from different places. Refrigeration, transport and online delivery can also change how food reaches the table. In the past, choices often depended more on local and seasonal supplies. Still, traditional dishes remain important, so change does not mean that older food habits simply disappear.

中文对照

如今很多人能接触更丰富的食材和各地菜肴。冷藏、运输和线上配送也改变食物上桌的方式。过去的选择往往更依赖本地和季节供应。不过，传统菜仍然重要，变化并不意味着旧饮食习惯直接消失。

关键词

a wider range · seasonal supplies · traditional dishes
