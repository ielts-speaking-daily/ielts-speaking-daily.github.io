英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A childhood friend I would like to talk about is a classmate from primary school in my hometown, a small county town in north-eastern China. We first met when we sat near each other in class and began talking during breaks. We often spent time together after lessons. Sometimes we exchanged books or discussed something we had watched. On other days, we simply walked home and talked about what had happened at school. We didn't need an expensive activity to have a good time. What I liked most was that they were easy to talk to. If I wasn't ready to speak, they didn't force me to join a noisy conversation. But when we were comfortable together, we could talk for a long time about small things. They also had a sense of humour that made an ordinary school day feel lighter. The friendship was important because it gave me a familiar person outside my family. We could share interests and help each other when a school task felt difficult. Looking back, I value the simple consistency of it: seeing someone regularly, listening to them, and gradually building trust. That's still the kind of friendship I find most comfortable.

中文对照

我想谈的一位童年朋友，是家乡小学里的一位同学。我的家乡是中国东北的一个小县城。我们最初认识，是因为上课时座位离得很近，课间便开始聊天。 放学后，我们经常一起待一会儿。有时会交换书，或者讨论看过的内容。还有些时候，我们只是一起走回家，聊聊学校里发生的事。不需要参加昂贵的活动，我们也能过得很开心。 我最喜欢的一点，是与对方聊天很自在。如果我一时不想开口，对方不会逼我加入热闹的谈话。但当我们相处得很放松时，也能围绕小事聊很久。对方还有幽默感，让普通的上学日也显得轻松些。 这段友谊很重要，因为它让我在家人之外还有一个熟悉的人。我们可以分享兴趣，遇到难做的学习任务时也会互相帮助。回头看，我珍惜的是这种简单而稳定的相处：经常见面、倾听彼此、慢慢建立信任。这仍然是让我最自在的一种友谊。

They started thinking seriously about the medical field near the end of school, when choosing what to study became more immediate. They were interested in science, but they also wanted work that involved direct contact with people rather than only studying information from a distance. Their goal is to train as a nurse and work in a hospital. What attracts them is the practical side: noticing what a patient needs, explaining simple information clearly, and working with other professionals. They understand that the job can involve difficult schedules and responsibility, so it isn't simply an idealized picture of helping people. I think the choice suits the qualities they want to develop. They are patient and prefer doing something concrete when someone needs support. My own direction is AI and robotics, so our paths are different. But I can understand the wish to connect knowledge with real situations. I would hope they choose it after learning enough about the everyday work, not just because the title sounds respectable.
