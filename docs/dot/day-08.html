擅长做手工的人

原题与完整提示

Describe a person who is good at making things by hand You should say: Who this person is What he/she makes What materials or technologies he/she uses And why you think this person is good at making things by hand

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

A person who is good at making things by hand is a friend from a robotics project. We met through our studies, and I noticed their practical skills while we were working on a small prototype together. They make simple robot models and useful little parts for experiments. They use materials such as plastic pieces, wires and small electronic boards, together with basic hand tools. Sometimes they also use computer design software before assembling everything. What impressed me was the way they checked how each part fitted. They didn't simply put the pieces together and hope for the best. If something didn't line up, they adjusted the design and tested it again. The finished object looked simple, but a lot of careful work went into it. I think they're good at this because they are patient and understand both the design and the practical limits of the materials. I enjoy programming, so it's interesting to see someone turn an idea into a physical object. It reminds me that robotics needs practical skills as well as good software.

中文对照

一位擅长手工制作的人，是机器人项目中的一位朋友。我们通过学习认识，我在一起制作小原型时注意到了对方的动手能力。 这位朋友制作简单机器人模型，以及实验中实用的小零件。材料包括塑料部件、电线和小电路板，也会用基本手工工具。有时会先用电脑设计软件，再进行组装。 让我印象深刻的是，对方会检查每个部件如何配合，而不是把东西拼起来后碰运气。如果某处对不齐，就调整设计并重新测试。成品看起来简单，但背后有很多细致工作。 我认为这位朋友擅长制作，是因为有耐心，也同时理解设计和材料的实际限制。我喜欢编程，因此很有兴趣看别人把想法变成实物。它提醒我，机器人除了好的软件，还需要动手能力。

关键词

robotics friend · small prototype · materials and tools · check the fit · ideas into objects

待确认素材与观点

  • 机器人项目朋友、原型、材料和合作活动全部为候选

原题与完整提示

Describe an enjoyable evening you had with your friends You should say: When and where it was What you did Who you spent the evening with And explain why it was enjoyable

英文参考答案（非用户原答）

An enjoyable evening I'd describe was a quiet dinner with two close friends. We met at a small restaurant one weekend, after a period when we'd all been busy with our studies. The plan was simple: have a meal and catch up properly. We talked about everyday things rather than trying to discuss serious work the whole time. I mentioned some anime I'd been interested in, and we also shared stories about things that had happened recently. The conversation moved naturally from one topic to another. After dinner, we took a short walk instead of leaving immediately. There wasn't a particular attraction we needed to see. We just continued chatting and enjoyed having time together without a deadline. It was enjoyable because I felt relaxed with people who knew me well. I'm usually quiet, but I'm more talkative with close friends, so a small gathering suits me better than a very loud party. I remember the evening for the comfortable conversation, rather than for anything expensive or dramatic. It was a useful reminder that spending time with friends can be simple.

中文对照

我想描述的一个愉快的夜晚，是和两位好友安静地吃晚饭。那是一个周末，我们在一家小餐馆见面，之前大家都忙于学习。计划很简单：吃顿饭，好好聊聊近况。 我们谈日常话题，而不是一直讨论严肃的工作。我提到一些感兴趣的动漫，也彼此分享最近发生的事情。话题自然地从一个转向另一个。 饭后，我们没有马上离开，而是短暂散步。没有必须看的景点，只是继续聊天，享受不用赶时间的相处。 这个夜晚愉快，是因为与熟悉的人在一起，我感到放松。我通常安静，但在好友面前比较健谈，因此小聚会比很吵的派对更适合我。我记住的是自在的交谈，而不是昂贵的消费或令人惊奇的事情。它提醒我，与朋友相处可以很简单。

关键词

two close friends · weekend dinner · anime and daily life · short walk · comfortable conversation

待确认素材与观点

  • 具体朋友、时间、餐厅、饭后散步为候选；安静但熟人前健谈和动漫兴趣已确认
